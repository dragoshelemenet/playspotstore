PlaySpotStore V44 — Moldova-wide LOCAL SEO, no city spam
====================================================

Scope: 279+ existing products across 7 categories, 2 languages (RO/RU).
Updates existing homepages, 14 category pages, and all product pages in initial
indexable HTML, as well as a repeat-safe V29 regeneration hook.

SEO intentions:
- Individual product and platform names in page titles and meta descriptions.
- Local in-person handover by arrangement in Chisinau; courier delivery elsewhere
  across Moldova. Information is visible to customers, not hidden SEO copy.
- A single natural overview on homepages mentions Balti, Orhei, Ungheni, Cahul,
  Soroca and Comrat as examples, not 300 autogenerated city landing pages.
- Existing bilingual canonical/hreflang and all product
  JSON-LD are kept intact; two original, linked RO/RU shopping and delivery guides
  are included in the sitemap, avoiding fabricated condition/stock/reviews/ratings,
  checkout capabilities, shipping prices, physical shop address or times.
- Neither 'new' nor 'used' is assigned automatically to individual inventory.
  In each case the buyer should confirm real item condition by contacting seller.
- Existing V42 editorial impressions, V36/V37 category search, V39 motion,
  pricing, photos, and all existing URLs are untouched.

SEO-friendly new URLs (one guide per language, no doorways):
  /jocuri-video-chisinau-moldova.html
  /ru/videoigry-kishinev-moldova.html
These are original informational/commercial service pages, not cloned categories.
They are linked from home, categories and products and entered in both the XML and
HTML sitemaps. Current product/category URLs are stable, containing real platform
and product words. Do not change old URLs without server-side 301 redirects.

No 'Sonic 5 PS4' is invented. Search pages can match only products in catalog.
Google may recrawl/reindex but no rankings/indexing timing are guaranteed.

Installation in GitHub Codespaces: upload this ZIP to the repo main, then run:
  git pull --ff-only origin main && unzip -o playspotstore-v44-moldova-local-seo.zip -d . && bash playspot-v44/install.sh

The script refuses to run on dirty tracked files and never resets/reverts work.
After installation, use Google Search Console to submit the existing sitemap
https://playspotstore.online/sitemap.xml and inspect representative category and
product URLs. No separate sitemap URLs were artificially generated for cities.
